* Scope
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: scope
:END:

The contract between ~debounce~ and its two neighbours: the pin that drives
~din~ and the logic that reads ~dout~.  The block has no software interface.
It turns a raw, asynchronous contact (button, switch) into a signal that is
synchronous to ~clk~ and changes only after the contact has rested.

- Block :: ~src/debounce.vhd~
- Version :: 1.0.0
- Requirements :: [[file:debounce_srs.org::#requirements][debounce requirements]]
- Design :: [[file:debounce_hdd.org::#overview][debounce design description]]
- Enforced by :: ~tb/tb_debounce.vhd~; the ~p_tw_*~ processes are tripwire
  monitors, one per clause below

** Generics and derived numbers
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: numbers
:END:

Everything below is counted in clocks and follows from one constant of the
design:

#+BEGIN_EXAMPLE
C_LIMIT = G_CLK_HZ / 1000 * G_DEBOUNCE_MS     (integer arithmetic, divided first)
#+END_EXAMPLE

| Name     | Value       | Meaning                                              |
|----------+-------------+------------------------------------------------------|
| SAMPLES  | C_LIMIT + 1 | equal samples of ~din~ needed before ~dout~ changes  |
| LATENCY  | C_LIMIT + 3 | clock edges from a change of ~din~ to that of ~dout~ |
| INTERVAL | C_LIMIT + 1 | least number of clocks between two changes of ~dout~ |

With the defaults (100 MHz, 10 ms) C_LIMIT is 1 000 000, so the contact
must rest for 1 000 001 clocks: 10 ns longer than the 10 ms asked for.

Two limits of the arithmetic:

- ~G_CLK_HZ~ below 1000 makes C_LIMIT zero, which the design rejects when
  it is elaborated (range error on a ~positive~ constant).
- ~G_CLK_HZ~ is cut to whole kHz, so 32 768 Hz counts as 32 000 Hz and the
  time comes out 2.3 % short.

** Clocks and resets
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: clocks
:END:

| Clock | Frequency  | Source       | Drives          |
|-------+------------+--------------+-----------------|
| clk   | ~G_CLK_HZ~ | system clock | the whole block |

| Reset | Active | Synchronous to | Minimum length | Resets                 |
|-------+--------+----------------+----------------+------------------------|
| rst   | high   | clk            | 1 clock        | ~dout~ and the counter |

~rst~ does not reset the two synchronizer flip-flops; they keep following
~din~.  See [[#reset][Reset]] for what that means at the port.

** Clock domain crossings
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: cdc
:END:

| From clock     | To clock | Signal(s) | Structure                        | Constraint                       |
|----------------+----------+-----------+----------------------------------+----------------------------------|
| none (the pin) | clk      | ~din~     | two flip-flops inside this block | false path or input delay on din |

The crossing belongs to this block: do not put another synchronizer in
front of it.  The constraint belongs to the project and is not part of this
example.

* Timing contracts
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: timing
:END:

~din~ is sampled at every rising edge of ~clk~.  A sample is the level of
~din~ at that edge; what it does between two edges is not seen.

** Filter
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: filter
:END:

~dout~ takes a level only after ~din~ showed that level at SAMPLES
consecutive clock edges.  One sample of the other level starts the count
again.

In time, for a pulse on ~din~ of width W and a clock period T:

| W                      | Result                                |
|------------------------+---------------------------------------|
| W <= C_LIMIT x T       | never passed on                       |
| W >= (C_LIMIT + 1) x T | always passed on                      |
| between the two        | depends on where the clock edges fall |

An input that never rests for SAMPLES clocks is never passed on, however
long that goes on.  That is the purpose of the block, not a fault.

- Enforced by :: ~p_tw_filter~

** Latency
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: latency
:END:

When ~din~ changes and then rests, ~dout~ follows at the LATENCY-th rising
edge after the change: the first two edges move the level through the
synchronizer, the next SAMPLES count it.  In time that is more than
(C_LIMIT + 2) and at most (C_LIMIT + 3) clock periods.

- Enforced by :: ~p_tw_latency~

** Minimum interval
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: min-interval
:END:

Two changes of ~dout~ are at least INTERVAL clocks apart, so a counter or
an edge detector behind it never sees a pulse shorter than that.  A change
of ~din~ taken back as soon as it was passed on is ignored like any other
short pulse.

- Enforced by :: ~p_tw_min_interval~

** Reset
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: reset
:END:

~dout~ is low from the clock after ~rst~ is sampled high and stays low
while ~rst~ is high, whatever ~din~ does.  The count starts again when
~rst~ is released.

A contact that is closed during reset therefore reads as open first:
~dout~ rises SAMPLES clocks after ~rst~ is released.  Logic that acts on a
rising ~dout~ sees an edge that the contact did not make.

- Enforced by :: ~p_tw_reset~

** What the neighbours may do
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: illegal-sequences
:END:

There is no illegal input: ~din~ may do anything at any time.  The table
lists the behaviour that looks like a fault from outside and is not.

| The input does                          | The block does                         | Checked by |
|-----------------------------------------+----------------------------------------+------------|
| Bounces, each rest shorter than SAMPLES | Nothing, then one change when it rests | [[file:debounce_vplan.org::#TC-10][TC-10]]      |
| Glitches at any phase of the clock      | Nothing                                | [[file:debounce_vplan.org::#TC-11][TC-11]]      |
| Toggles without ever resting            | Nothing, for as long as it lasts       | [[file:debounce_vplan.org::#TC-12][TC-12]]      |
| Changes back as ~dout~ changes          | Keeps the new level                    | [[file:debounce_vplan.org::#TC-14][TC-14]]      |
| ~rst~ while a count is running          | Starts the count again                 | [[file:debounce_vplan.org::#TC-13][TC-13]]      |

* Error and recovery
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: errors
:END:

The block reports no error and cannot lock up: every state is left by
~rst~ or by a resting input.  It keeps no state that a software restart
could leave stale.

A sample taken while ~din~ is changing can settle either way.  The two
synchronizer flip-flops give it a clock to settle; the filter then treats
it as one more bounce.  Metastability is not simulated and the settling
time was not analysed for a device.

* Change control
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: change-control
:END:

A change to a number in [[#numbers][Generics and derived numbers]] changes the
constants at the top of ~tb/tb_debounce.vhd~ in the same commit; the
monitors take their limits from those constants.

| Consumer            | Uses                             | Contact |
|---------------------+----------------------------------+---------|
| Logic behind ~dout~ | latency, minimum interval, reset |         |
| Top level           | ~din~ pin and its constraint     |         |
