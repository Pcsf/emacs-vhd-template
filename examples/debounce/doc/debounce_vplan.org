| Generics     | ~G_CLK_HZ~ = 8000, ~G_DEBOUNCE_MS~ = 1: C_LIMIT = 8     |
| Clock period | 10 ns                                                   |

The small C_LIMIT keeps the run short; every limit in the testbench is
written in terms of C_LIMIT.  ~din~ moves 3 ns after a clock edge in most
tests and at nine other phases in TC-11.  Outputs are checked at the
falling edge.

* Contract monitors
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: monitors
:END:

Tripwire monitors: independent processes in ~tb_debounce~ that drive
nothing and raise an error when their clause is broken.  They sample ~din~
and ~rst~ at the rising edge, as the design does, and check at the falling
edge.

All four clauses are promises of the hardware, so no test of the correct
design makes a monitor fire.  Each one is shown to work by a mutant of the
design in the next section.

| Monitor             | ICD clause       | Fires in        |
|---------------------+------------------+-----------------|
| ~p_tw_filter~       | [[file:debounce_icd.org::#filter][filter]]           | mutants 1, 2, 3 |
| ~p_tw_latency~      | [[file:debounce_icd.org::#latency][latency]]          | mutant 4        |
| ~p_tw_min_interval~ | [[file:debounce_icd.org::#min-interval][minimum interval]] | mutant 5        |
| ~p_tw_reset~        | [[file:debounce_icd.org::#reset][reset]]            | mutant 6        |

* Mutation checks
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: mutations
:END:

Each row is a line of ~tb/mutants.txt~: the design is changed, the same
testbench is run, and the run must fail with the monitor named.  The runner
refuses a mutant that changes nothing and one that survives.

| # | Mutant                  | Change to the design                 | Killed by           | First test to fail |
|---+-------------------------+--------------------------------------+---------------------+--------------------|
| 1 | ~one_sample_short~      | level taken at ~cnt = C_LIMIT - 1~   | ~p_tw_filter~       | TC-02              |
| 2 | ~count_not_cleared~     | count kept when the input returns    | ~p_tw_filter~       | TC-05              |
| 3 | ~synchronizer_bypassed~ | compare ~sync(0)~, one stage only    | ~p_tw_filter~       | TC-02              |
| 4 | ~never_follows~         | ~stable~ never updated               | ~p_tw_latency~      | TC-02              |
| 5 | ~rearmed_after_change~  | count left at C_LIMIT after a change | ~p_tw_min_interval~ | TC-05              |
| 6 | ~reset_value~           | ~stable~ reset to 1                  | ~p_tw_reset~        | TC-01              |

Mutant 2 passes TC-04, the single short pulse: only a second pulse shows
that the count was kept.  That is why TC-10 and TC-12 exist.
